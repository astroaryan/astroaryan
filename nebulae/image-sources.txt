NEBULA INFORMATION AND IMAGE SOURCES — revised 2026-10-28

This directory currently contains 30 objects; all are documented below.
Nebula info links describe the object. Image source links document the actual image used; these are distinct when different photographs are shown on the information page.
Stellarium screenshots retain the photographer credits supplied by its texture catalog. Where the original image URL was missing or unavailable, the image source links to its Stellarium catalog entry.
External photographs retain their original framing and colours; conversion to PNG and proportional downsampling only. Their full image credits and license links are displayed below the game image.
Reuse licenses for the remaining Stellarium textures have not been independently verified.
