Physics 2 Astrophysics

I co-founded Physics 2 Astrophysics, a free online summer program designed to introduce high school students to astronomy. In high school, I had limited access to astronomy classes, competitions, or research opportunities that would help me understand what it’s like to study astrophysics in college. At P2A, we wanted to provide that introduction while allowing students to balance work, family responsibilities, and other interests. Our only requirements are a background in Algebra 2 and a genuine interest in learning.

Over our first three summers, more than 70 students have graduated from P2A, many of whom now study STEM in college. The five-week program takes about 15 hours a week and is taught by undergrads majoring in astro(physics). Students learn Physics on Mondays, apply it 2 Astronomy on Wednesdays, and code Python on Fridays. They work together on problem sets covering solar systems, stars, galaxies, and cosmology, and finish by presenting their own mini research projects.
