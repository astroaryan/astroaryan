Brown dwarf research assets

Brown dwarf illustration: supplied by the website owner.
The cover embeds the supplied illustration and two unaltered plot crops as a single SVG image.

Scientific figures: Gupta et al., "Estimating M Dwarf Ages using their Transiting Brown Dwarf Companions", draft dated October 26, 2026, supplied by the website owner.
- brown-dwarf-cover.svg: Figure 3 panels (e) and (f), page 9, stacked vertically beside the illustration. The screenshot supplied with the request is from Figure 3, not Figure 2.
- brown-dwarf-figure-7.png: Figure 7, page 20, with the complete three-panel graphic.
- brown-dwarf-figure-3.png: Figure 3, page 9, with all six panels.
Plots were rendered directly from the PDF with Poppler at a page height of 3200 pixels, then cropped to their panels. Data, axes, legends, and labels were preserved.

Age ranges follow the paper's abstract: model-dependent median ages of 1.3-2.9 Gyr for LP 261-75C and 0.4-1.3 Gyr for AD 3116B.
General brown dwarf mass range reference: https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/what-is-a-brown-dwarf/
