Planet equilibrium temperature

How warm is a planet? Change its star, orbit, and reflectivity to explore.

Drag the planet left or right to change its distance. You can also use the sliders or enter exact values.

Playback: 12 seconds per orbit, with Keplerian relative speed. Time is illustrative.

Blackbody spectrum

Explore the surface spectral flux of a blackbody as its temperature changes.

Adjust the slider or enter an exact temperature. The wavelength and flux axes rescale with temperature.

Curve colour approximates the blackbody's visible colour. Brightness is normalized and colours outside the display gamut are clipped; very cool blackbodies emit almost no visible light.
